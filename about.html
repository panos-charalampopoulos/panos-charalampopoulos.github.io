About

My name is Panos Charalampopoulos and I come from Athens, Greece. I am currently a Beatriu de Pinós Postdoctoral Fellow, working at ICE-CSIC in Barcelona, Spain. Before this, I was a postdoc at the University of Turku, and I completed my PhD at DTU Space (Copenhagen, Denmark) under the supervision of Giorgos Leloudas. I did my MSc at the Niels Bohr Institute and worked on my MSc thesis at the DARK Cosmology Center (supervised by Katie Auchettl). I received my BSc in Physics from the National and Kapodistrian University of Athens.

Research interests

My area of study is astrophysics of transient sources like tidal disruption events, supernovae and kilonovae. I use photo- metric and spectroscopic observations in the optical wavelengths, as well as X-rays, ultraviolet, and infrared. My skills cover discovering, classifying and following-up extragalactic transients. I also use polarimetry to study the geometries of transients.
